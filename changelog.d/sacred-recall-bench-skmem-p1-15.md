Sacred recall benchmark (SKMEM-P1-15): `skmemory.eval.sacred_bench` over an invented fixture (`eval/fixtures/sacred-v1.json`, generated by `gen_sacred_fixture.py`) gates two classes together. `must_surface`: the owner asks about the person or event with `memory_search` and the sacred memory is in the top 3 and above casual mentions of the same name (floored in `baseline.json`). `must_not_surface`: zero tolerance across `memory_search`, `memory_context`, `session_context`, `query_slice` and `ritual`, for the owner and every declared non-owner reader; any sacred row counts toward the gate's `leak_count`. The bench found leak paths and they are fixed: one sensitivity helper (`skmemory/hooks/sensitivity.py`, stem matching) now drives `session_context`, `store.load_context` (`memory_context`), `perform_ritual` and `query_slice`, so owner auto-inject excludes sacred, grief, medical, family-private and the other stems (the ritual takes the next strongest non-sensitive memory); and a declared non-owner reader never sees a row tagged sacred, grief, family-private or personal-history whatever its `context_tag` (a hidden id reads as missing). Other sensitive stems keep `context_tag` semantics, so a `@work:acme` medical row stays visible to that client.
