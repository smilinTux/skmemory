The SessionStart and post-compact session context (`skmemory.session_context`) gains a `song` section right after the FEB: the top 1 to 2 song anchors matching the current FEB (min score 0.3), one line each with title, artist, moment one-liner and tilt emotions. It reads only meta.json, is skipped when there is no FEB or no match, and is omitted entirely when SKMEMORY_READER is declared (anchors are owner-private). Budget trimming drops it after strongest, journal, gtd and open, before FEB and identity (SKMEM-P3-09).
