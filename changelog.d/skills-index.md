A read-only skill index (`skmemory.skills_index`, SKMEM-P4-03) covers the hermes, clawd, claude, plugin-cache and skcapstone skill stores: name, description, content hash, store. `skmemory skills search <query>` ranks lexical match times the first-party ledger score, puts anti-patterns last and flags them and served-never-followed skills, and shows imported Hermes counters apart. `skmemory skills index` reports per-store counts and cross-store name collisions (same name, different hash). Nothing is moved, edited or deleted; paths are configurable with `--store NAME=PATH` and missing directories are skipped.
