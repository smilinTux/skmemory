The fused semantic arm (`PGVectorBackend.search_semantic`) now sets `hnsw.ef_search` (never below the arm limit, so a LIMIT of 90 is not capped at the default 40 candidates) and `hnsw.iterative_scan` with `SET LOCAL` inside its own query transaction, so a filtered kNN keeps scanning until the WHERE clause is satisfied and no connection is left tuned. Configurable with `SKMEMORY_HNSW_EF_SEARCH` and `SKMEMORY_HNSW_ITERATIVE_SCAN` (SKMEM-P1-10).
