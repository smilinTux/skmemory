Added: `skmemory fortress reseal`, a re-seal for memories proven to be victims of a fixed sealing bug (a seal taken over a title with whitespace `Memory` strips on load, or a promoted copy that kept its source's hash). Dry run by default: it lists each id, the reason, the old and new hash and the proof, and writes nothing. `--write` re-seals, re-proves each memory at write time, and logs every re-seal (`fortress-reseal.jsonl` and the audit chain). Edited content, changed titles, foreign hashes, failing parents and signed files are never touched.
